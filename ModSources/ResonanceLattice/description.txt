Resonance Lattice — a reusable gameplay library for tModLoader (stable, .NET 8) that adds a placeable energy grid to Terraria. Place Resonant Crystals to auto-link them into networks that carry aether charge: feed them with Aether Cells or Stormcaller bolts, read them with the Lattice Probe, summon a Lattice Wisp that drains and weaponizes the charge, and watch overloaded networks surge into lightning that zaps nearby enemies. Fully server-authoritative in multiplayer with request validation, world-save persistence and bounded simulation/network budgets.
